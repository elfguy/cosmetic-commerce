# 상세 13 — 제품 정보 / 전성분

Topic: readable product info and ingredient information. Text-heavy but must still look designed, not plain table only.

Required Korean copy:
Headline: 제품 정보와 전성분을 확인하세요
Product info rows:
제품명: 유어스킨플러스 히알루론산 토너
용량: 500ml
사용방법: 세안 후 손 또는 화장솜에 적당량을 덜어 피부결을 따라 사용하세요
판매·제조: (주)유어스킨

Ingredient section title: 전성분
Include a compact readable ingredient block impression, not necessarily every tiny ingredient perfectly, but Korean must not be garbled.

Visual: premium info card layout with soft aqua background, clean typography, small product/water motif. Avoid overcrowding.

Global style and safety rules for every cut:
- Create ONE premium Korean Coupang mobile detail-page image, vertical 780:1360 composition with safe margins.
- Match approved toner V1 sibling cuts: clean white base, soft aqua/mint green accents, rounded cards, airy Korean skincare ad quality.
- Use watery transparent toner visuals, not creamy lotion.
- Korean text must be large, clean, naturally spelled, not garbled.
- No visible version markers: no V1/V2/V3/V4, no renewal/proof/candidate, no CUT/DETAIL CUT, no POINT/STEP, no big numeric badges.
- Avoid medical/absolute claims. Do NOT use: 피부 깊은 곳까지, 속부터 충전, 피부 장벽 강화, 진정 효과, 민감 피부도 안심, 안전한 성분만, 트러블 완화, 치료, 재생, 회복.
- Do not invent fake certificates, fake official logos, fake boxes, or fake packaging.
- If the product appears, preserve YOURSKIN+ HYALURONIC ACID TONER 500ml identity and do not redesign the label.
